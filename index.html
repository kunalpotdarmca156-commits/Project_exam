What is CET?

A Common Entrance Test, run entirely online.

Colleges use a CET to admit students fairly, based on a single standardised exam instead of a hundred different processes. CrustExams gives every college its own private space to run that test — from onboarding its own admin, to teachers writing question papers, to students logging in and getting scored automatically.

Every college that joins CrustExams works inside its own account. Nothing is shared between colleges — except the platform that makes it all run.

01

College Admin sets up

Registers the college, adds teacher accounts, adds student accounts, and defines who can create tests.

02

Teachers build the CET

Create question papers, set duration and marking rules, and assign the test to specific batches of students.

03

Students take the test

Log in, attempt the CET within the given time, and get results the moment the test is submitted.

Three panels, one platform

Built around who does what.

CrustExams isn't one dashboard trying to be everything — it's three focused panels, each built for the person using it.

College Admin

The account that owns the college's space on CrustExams and keeps it running.

  • Register the college on CrustExams
  • Add and remove Teacher accounts
  • Add and remove Student accounts
  • Oversee every CET held by the college
Manages the college

Teacher

Added by their College Admin, teachers are the ones who actually build each CET.

  • Create question papers for a CET
  • Set duration, marks and cut-offs
  • Assign tests to student batches
  • Review scores once a test closes
Creates the test

Student

Added by their College Admin, students log in only to attempt the CET assigned to them.

  • Log in with college-issued credentials
  • Attempt the CET within the time limit
  • Get results the moment it's submitted
  • Nothing to configure — just log in and write

Why CrustExams

Everything a CET actually needs.

Universal onboarding
